## About

Electronics & Communication Engineering undergraduate (3rd year, CGPA 8.2) at Arya College of Engineering & IT, Jaipur. My work spans the full robotics stack: bare-metal firmware, ROS 2 middleware, deep learning perception, and production-grade software architecture.

I believe impactful robotics does not require expensive resources — it requires smart design, modular engineering, and a clear understanding of the problem-solution fit. My goal is to build systems that are reliable, field-deployable, and upgrade-friendly under real-world constraints.

**Currently working on:** Nav2 + SLAM Toolbox integration with my Kinect ROS 2 driver · MoveIt 2 for 6-DOF arm planning · Gazebo simulation pipeline for SPARK hexapod.
